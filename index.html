FAQ

Common questions

What do I need before my first lesson?

You'll need a valid UK provisional driving licence. You can apply for one on GOV.UK from the age of 15 years and 9 months, and start driving lessons once you're 17.

Do you teach in automatic cars?

Yes, all our lessons are in an automatic car. There's no clutch or gears to worry about, so many learners pick it up faster. If you pass in an automatic, your licence will cover automatic cars only.

How many lessons will I need?

Everyone learns at a different pace. DVSA figures suggest learners need around 45 hours of professional lessons plus 22 hours of private practice on average. We'll give you an honest assessment after your first lesson.

Do I need to pass my theory test first?

You can start lessons straight away, but you must pass your theory test before you can book your practical driving test. We're happy to help you plan both.

Do you offer lessons in the evenings or at weekends?

Yes, we offer flexible scheduling including evenings and weekends to fit around school, college or work.

Do you offer block booking discounts?

Yes. A block of 10 one-hour lessons is £430, which saves you £20. Get in touch by phone or WhatsApp if you'd like a price for an intensive course.
